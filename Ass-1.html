About Department

The Department of Information Technology at K. K. Wagh Institute of Engineering Education and Research offers a B.Tech programme in Information Technology.

The department focuses on technical knowledge, practical learning, research and development of professional skills.

Programme Offered: B.Tech in Information Technology

Official Department Website

Vision

To provide an enriched academic environment with quality research in Information Technology.

Mission

  • To impart fundamental knowledge.
  • To make students familiar with recent IT trends.
  • To develop responsible IT professionals.
  • To encourage professional and social ethics.
